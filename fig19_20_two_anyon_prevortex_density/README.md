# Figs. 19 and 20: flux-dressed two-anyon states before vortex attachment

Density `2 pi rho(x,0) lstar^2` of the flux-dressed two-QP (Fig. 19) and two-QH (Fig. 20) effective states with the vortex factor removed, one anyon at the origin (k1 = 0) and the other at omega = 10 lstar in the k state. Exact (quadrature, Mmax = 140).

Files: `two_anyon_prevortex_density_qp.csv`, `two_anyon_prevortex_density_qh.csv`.

Columns: `x_over_lstar`, then `rho_alpha{0,0p2857,0p4,0p6667}_k{0,1,2}` for alpha = 0, 2/7, 2/5, 2/3 and k = 0, 1, 2; alpha = 0 is the noninteracting reference. The curves in the figure are shifted vertically for display; the values here are unshifted.

Script: `scripts/plot_prevortex_for_paper.jl` (repository `two_anyon_hf`).
