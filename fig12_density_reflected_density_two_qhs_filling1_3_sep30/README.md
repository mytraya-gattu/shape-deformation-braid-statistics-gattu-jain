# Fig. 12: two QHs at nu = 1/3 at larger separation

Density `2 pi rho(x,0) lB^2` along the line joining two quasiholes at nu = 1/3, N = 144, for separations 12 lB, 24 lB, 30 lB, with k1 = 0 and k2 = 0, 1, 2 (conventional CF wave functions). Results obtained via Monte Carlo integration.

## Files
`density_N144_nu1_3_omega{12.0,24.0,30.0}_k10_k2{0,1,2}_conventional.csv` (`omega` is the total separation in lB).

## Columns
1. `x / lB`: position along the cut
2. `2 pi rho(x,0) lB^2`: density (mean)
3. `Delta (2 pi rho)`: standard error
