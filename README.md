# shape-deformation-braid-statistics-gattu-jain

Numerical data generated for the manuscript *Shape Deformation and Braid Statistics of Fractional Quantum Hall Quasiparticles* (Mytraya Gattu and Jainendra K. Jain, 2026, arXiv:2609.XXXXX), presented in Figs. 3, 4, 6, 7, and 9–27. The remaining figures of the manuscript (Figs. 1, 2, 5, and 8) are schematic illustrations. (The density curves shown in Fig. 8 are the same as the nu = 1/3 curves of Fig. 9 and can be found in the folder for Figs. 9 and 10.) The data are organized by the figure in which they appear: the folder name starts with the figure number and continues with the name of the figure file in the manuscript source. For example, the data of Fig. 12 are in `fig12_density_reflected_density_two_qhs_filling1_3_sep30`, and the data of Figs. 9 and 10 are in `fig09_10_single_qh_qp_density_and_charge`.

Lengths are in units of the magnetic length `lB`, or of the effective magnetic length `lstar` for the two-anyon model (Figs. 3, 4, 16, 19, 20, 21, and 27); densities are reported in units of the filling factor, i.e., as `2 pi rho lB^2` or `2 pi rho lstar^2`. For observables obtained by Monte Carlo integration we report both the mean and its standard error; for observables obtained by quadrature or analytically we report only the value. Further details (source scripts, number of Monte Carlo chains, conventions) are given in the README of each folder.
