# Figs. 9 and 10: single QH and QP density and integrated charge

Radial density `2 pi rho(r) lB^2` and integrated charge `Q(r)` (in units of electron charge e) for a single localized quasihole (QH) or quasiparticle (QP) at nu = 1/3 (N = 64) and nu = 2/5 (N = 80), with defect angular momentum m = 0, 1, 2. (Fig. 8 shows the nu = 1/3 curves from here.) Results obtained via Monte Carlo integration.

## Files
`{qh,qp}_nu{1_3,2_5}_mdefect_{0,1,2}.csv`

## Columns
1. `r / lB`: radial distance from the defect
2. `2 pi rho(r) lB^2`: density (mean)
3. `Delta (2 pi rho)`: standard error on density
4. `Q(r)`: integrated excess charge (mean)
5. `Delta Q`: standard error on Q(r)
